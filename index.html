frequently asked question

What tree services do you offer?
We offer a complete range of professional tree services, including tree removal, tree trimming, pruning, storm cleanup, and stump grinding. Whether you need routine maintenance or hazardous tree removal, our experienced team is here to help.

When should I schedule a tree removal service?
You should consider tree removal services if a tree is dead, diseased, leaning dangerously, or causing structural concerns. Removing unsafe trees promptly helps protect your property and ensures safety.

Do you provide stump grinding and stump removal?
Yes, we specialize in stump grinding and stump removal to completely eliminate unwanted tree stumps. This improves your yard’s appearance and prevents pests, regrowth, and tripping hazards.

What are the benefits of professional stump grinding?
Stump grinding services help restore your landscape by removing unsightly stumps, improving curb appeal, and making lawn care easier. It also frees up space for new landscaping or planting.

Can you help with land preparation?
Absolutely. We coordinate with reliable land grading companies to prepare your property, improve drainage, and ensure a solid foundation for landscaping or construction.

Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Stanton Tree Service is fully licensed and carries comprehensive liability and workers’ compensation insurance. This ensures that both your property and our team are fully protected throughout the entire process.

How much does tree removal or trimming cost?
Pricing depends on the tree’s size, species, location (proximity to power lines or buildings), and the equipment required. We provide free, no-obligation written estimates to give you an accurate price tailored to your specific needs.

Do you handle the cleanup and debris removal?

Absolutely. Our goal is to leave your property looking better than we found it. We chip the brush, remove all wood (unless you’d like to keep it for firewood), and rake the area clean so there’s no mess left behind
